Noise-Aware Dynamic Filtering for Motion Deblurring in Low-Light Images

Stevens Institute of Technology, 2026 · sole author, advised by Prof. Hong Man

In low-light blurred photos, sensor noise and real edges sit in the same frequency band, so deblurring networks either smear the edges or amplify the grain. NADF is a 2K parameter module that estimates a noise map and gates the high-frequency stream per pixel, taking a failing baseline from 8.56 dB to 19.34 dB PSNR and 0.238 to 0.647 SSIM on held-out images.

Stack PyTorch · MSFS-Net · LOL-Blur · PSNR and SSIM analysis

Three stages on one problem: an LSTM forecaster over household demand, an encoder-decoder extension that lifted accuracy 25 percent, and an actor-critic layer that reallocates power across sectors during a deficit, over 700,800 records generated across 200 households. Best Research Paper
